Issue 1: Per-item variant selections are ignored
Steps to reproduce (iPhone / Safari):
1. Open this product page:
   https://looty-japan.com/products/seat-hook-chair-back-hidden-small-rear-row-fur-creative-upgrade-seat-flip-back-row-object-storage-car-bag

- With 1 item (no discount), the checkout page opens immediately.
